# AMG Geospatial Domination Dashboard: Investment Pack Optimization Deep Research

**Target Audience/Engine:** Gemini Deep Research
**Objective:** Perform extreme, hedge-fund level due diligence on 5 specific real estate investment clusters ("Nodes") in the Marrakech region. The goal is to mathematically and qualitatively optimize these specific "Investment Packs" to absolute perfection from an investment standpoint, feeding into the AMG VDR (Virtual Data Room) investment model.

---

## CONTEXT & VDR INVESTMENT MODEL

You are acting as the Chief Investment Officer for AMG Building. You are preparing an executive presentation tool for the CEO to review 10-15 unit boutique luxury compounds.

Our VDR relies on the following rigid financial mechanics and metrics that you must optimize against:
- **Core Financials:** Total GDV (Gross Development Value), GDC (Gross Development Cost), and built-in wholesale vs. retail margin.
- **Return Metrics:** MOIC (Multiple on Invested Capital), Peak Equity Deployed, and **Combined IRR** (capital exit + periodic rental dividends).
- **Musharakah Waterfall:** A 4-tier GP/LP split (Tier 1: Return of Capital. Tier 2: 80/20 LP/GP to 1.25x. Tier 3: 60/40 GP promote).
- **Stress-Test Scenarios:** Bull (100% VEFA sell-through), Base (100% VEFA), Bear (50% VEFA, 45% rental occupancy pivot), and Catastrophic (0% VEFA, 30% rental occupancy floor).
- **Macro Drivers:** TPI (Taxe sur les Profits Immobiliers) applied only to VEFA gains, Opex Ratios (currently modeling 30-65%), and VNA (Vocation Non Agricole) friction.

---

## THE RESEARCH MANDATE

For each of the 5 Nodes and their respective Packs below, execute a comprehensive 5-Pillar deep dive.

### THE 5 RESEARCH PILLARS TO EVALUATE FOR EVERY PACK:
1. **Financial Viability & Arbitrage (GDC vs GDV):** Validate land costs (MAD/m2) and base build prices. Optimize the developer margin. How can we tighten OPEX to maximize net yields?
2. **Regulatory & VNA Reality:** Scrutinize the VNA (Vocation Non Agricole) clearance process for the specific zone. Is automatic clearance real, or is there hidden friction?
3. **Competitor Kill-Screen Validation:** I have provided a specific competitor/narrative to "crush" for each pack. Fact-check these claims. Identify the absolute weakest points of competing developments in that zone (density, utilities, visibility) to strengthen our narrative.
4. **Macro Infrastructure & Tourism (The 2030 World Cup Catalyst):** How exactly does local infrastructure (high-speed rail, airport expansions, stadium proximity, road upgrades) quantifiably impact the exit velocity (Days on Market) and asset liquidity?
5. **Institutional Exit Strategy:** Optimize the path for Dubai SPV funds or UAE capital. What specific yield or narrative (e.g., "ESG compliance", "Zero-impact") is required to make bulk 60-90 day flipping viable?
